About Lesotho Tourism

Discover the heritage, culture and spirit of the Kingdom in the Sky.

The Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a high-altitude, landlocked country encircled by South Africa. It is uniquely characterized by a network of highlands and rugged mountains, earning its famous title as the "Kingdom in the Sky" because its lowest point is 1,400 meters above sea level.

Our mission is to showcase the breathtaking natural beauty, rich Basotho culture, and warm hospitality of our nation to travelers from all around the globe.

Why Visit Us?

  • 🏔️ Unmatched mountain landscapes and pony-trekking trails
  • 🎨 Rich traditions featuring the iconic Basotho blanket and Mokorotlo hat
  • 🌊 Spectacular waterfalls like Maletsunyane Falls
  • 🧗 Thrilling outdoor adventures from hiking to 4x4 mountain passes
